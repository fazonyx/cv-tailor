# sources/

Put your raw material here: your current CV, your employer's competency file,
old applications, a photo, anything that describes what you have actually done.
The more detail, the better the profile your assistant can draft from it.

**Everything in this folder is git-ignored except this file**, and the supplied
`hooks/pre-commit` refuses a commit that stages anything from here. These are
your personal data; they have no business in a repository.

Once the files are in place:

    /extract-profile          (or: ask your assistant to read sources/
                               and draft profile/profile.yaml)
