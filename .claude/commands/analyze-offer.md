description: Read a job offer and give an honest verdict before writing anything
argument-hint: <path to offer.md, or paste the offer>
---

Read `$ARGUMENTS` (a job offer) and `profile/profile.yaml`. Do not write or
modify any file yet.

Answer, in this order and briefly:

1. **Verdict.** Worth applying, worth applying with reservations, or not worth
   it. Say which, in one sentence, before anything else.
2. **Direct hits.** Requirements covered by facts already in the profile. Quote
   the fact id for each.
3. **Transferable.** Requirements not met directly, but adjacent to something
   real. Name the neighbouring fact and how far the stretch is.
4. **Gaps.** Requirements with nothing behind them. Say for each whether it is
   disqualifying, addressable in the letter, or ignorable. Check them against
   `gaps:` in the profile.
5. **The angle.** In two or three lines: which facts should lead, which should
   be dropped, and what this employer is actually buying.

Do not soften the verdict. A well-argued "do not apply" saves more time than a
tailored CV for a job that will not happen.
