**Location** Lyon, France - around 30% travel to customer sites
**Contract** Permanent

## About

Quayside Systems sells a port operations platform to terminal operators across
Europe. Every deployment is a project: data models differ, processes differ,
and someone has to sit with the customer until the thing fits.

## What you will do

- Run discovery workshops with operations teams and turn what you hear into a
  scoped, deliverable backlog.
- Configure and extend the platform in Python for each deployment.
- Demo increments to customer stakeholders and collect the feedback that shapes
  the next one.
- Handle second-line support on live deployments; write the runbooks.
- Hand the system over to the customer's own team, documentation included.

## What we are looking for

- 3+ years in software engineering, with real customer exposure.
- Python and SQL; you can read and change a backend, not only describe it.
- Someone who is comfortable saying "that will not work" to a customer.
- Fluent English; French is a plus.

## Nice to have

- Presales experience.
- Logistics, port or transport domain knowledge.
