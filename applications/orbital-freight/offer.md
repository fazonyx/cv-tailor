## About

Orbital Freight tracks 60,000 vehicles for mid-size logistics operators. Our
platform ingests roughly 9,000 position updates per second and serves them back
to dispatch tools in near real time.

## What you will do

- Own services in the ingestion path: receive, validate, store and expose
  high-frequency telemetry.
- Keep read latency predictable as volume grows - profiling, query work,
  caching strategies.
- Design and evolve the REST APIs our customers' tools are built on.
- Take part in the on-call rotation for the services you own.
- Work with the data team on pipelines feeding our reporting product.

## What we are looking for

- 3+ years of backend engineering in Python.
- Solid relational database experience; time-series storage a plus.
- Comfortable with Docker and CI; Kubernetes experience appreciated.
- Experience with Kafka or a similar streaming platform is a plus.
- You write things down: runbooks, post-mortems, design notes.

## Nice to have

- Exposure to the logistics or mobility domain.
- Open-source contributions.
